Google Project Ara proposal
The Google Project Ara proposal aimed to revolutionize the smartphone industry by offering a
modular design that allowed users to easily replace or upgrade components like cameras, 
batteries, and processors. This approach was intended to extend the lifespan of devices and
reduce electronic waste. However, the project was ultimately abandoned due to technical challenges, 
high production costs, and pressure from existing smartphone manufacturers. Despite its potential, the project did not see the launch of a product, 
and its prototype devices were not released to the public.
